$$ \sum,\prod\\ \sum_{i=0}^n $$

括号

$$ [123]\\ (123) $$

$$ \{123\} $$

$$ (0,\frac 1 a]\\ \left(0,\frac 1 a\right] $$ $$ wrong: \frac {\partial f} {\partial x} |_x=0\\ right:\left.\frac {\partial f} {\partial x} \right|_x=0\\ %\frac {\partial f} {\partial x} \bigg|_x=0\\ $$

多行公式

$$ \begin{align} \text{wrong:} \quad a &= b+c+d \\ &= e+f \end{align} $$

$$ \begin{aligned} a &= b+c+d \\ &= e+f \end{aligned} $$

$$ \begin{align} a &= b+c+d \nonumber \\ &= e+f \end{align} $$
