希腊字母

$$ \begin{align} \alpha &\quad \beta \quad \gamma,\Gamma \\ \delta,\Delta &\quad \epsilon,\varepsilon \quad \zeta \\ \eta &\quad \theta,\vartheta,\Theta \quad \iota \\ \kappa &\quad \lambda,\Lambda \quad \mu \\ \nu &\quad \xi,\Xi \quad o \\ \pi,\varpi,\Pi &\quad \rho,\varrho \quad \sigma,\varsigma,\Sigma \\ \tau &\quad \upsilon,\Upsilon \quad \phi,\varphi,\Phi \\ \chi &\quad \psi,\Psi \quad \omega,\Omega \end{align} $$

$$ \begin{align} \varGamma &\quad \varDelta \quad \varTheta \quad \varLambda \\ \varXi &\quad \varPi \quad \varSigma \quad \varUpsilon \\ \varPhi &\quad \varPsi \quad \varOmega \end{align} $$

上下标

$$ a^2_2 \\ a_{ij} \\ x_i,x_{\rm i} \ %默认斜体，当你想要直立体时% {\text{A B},\rm{A B}} \\ {\text{ e i j}} \\ $$

分式与根式

$$ \frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2} $$

$$ \frac{\dfrac{1}{x} + 1}{x-1} $$

$$ \sqrt [3]{x} $$

普通运算符

$$ +-\\ \times,\cdot,\div\\ $$ $$ \pm,\mp\\ <,\ge,\le,\gg,\ll,> $$

$$ \ne,\approx,\equiv\\ \cap,\cup,\in,\notin,\subset,\subseteq,\subsetneq,\subsetneqq\\ \infty,\varnothing\\ \forall,\exists,\nexists\\ \because,\therefore\\ $$

$$ \mathbb R ,\R,\N,\Z,\mathbb Y\\ \mathcal F ,\mathscr F $$

$$ \cdots,\vdots,\ddots \ \partial, ∂(\text{opt+d}), \nabla\\ \propto,\degree $$

$$ \sin x,\sec x, \cosh x\\ \log_2 x,\ln_3 10,lg x\\ \lim_{x \to 0} \frac {x}{\sin x }\\ \max x $$

运算符号超过一个字母时应该使用直立体
$$ wrong:MSE(x)\\ right:\text{MSE}(x) $$

大型运算符

$$ \sum,\prod\\ \sum_{i=0}^n $$

$$ \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n x_i}{\sum_{i=1}^n x_i}\\ \frac{\sum\limits_{i=1}^n x_i}{\sum\limits_{i=1}^n x_i} $$

$$ \int,\iint,\iiint,\oint,\oiint \\ \int_{-\infty}^0 f(x), \text d x \\ 使用\backslash,提供一个小间隔，比较标准 $$

标注符号

$$ \vec x,\vec {xy},\overrightarrow {xy} \\ \bar x , \overline {xy} \\ \dot x, \hat x, \tilde x,\ddot x \\ \widehat {xy}, \widetilde {xy} $$

$$ \leftarrow, \rightarrow, \leftrightarrow,\downarrow,\uparrow \Leftarrow, \Rightarrow, \Leftrightarrow,\Downarrow,\Uparrow \ \Longleftrightarrow \ $$

括号

$$ [123]\\ (123)\\ {123}\\ $$ $$ (0,\frac 1 a]\\ \left(0,\frac 1 a\right] $$ $$ wrong: \frac {\partial f} {\partial x} |_x=0\\ right:\left.\frac {\partial f} {\partial x} \right|_x=0\\ %\frac {\partial f} {\partial x} \bigg|_x=0\\ $$

多行公式

$$ \begin{align} \text{wrong:} \quad a &= b+c+d \\ &= e+f \end{align} $$

$$ \begin{aligned} a &= b+c+d \\ &= e+f \end{aligned} $$

$$ \begin{align} a &= b+c+d \nonumber \\ &= e+f \end{align} $$

&(ampersand)

$$ \begin{equation} f(x)= \begin{cases} \sin x&, -\pi\le x \le\pi\\ 0&,\text{others} \end{cases} \end{equation} $$

矩阵

$$ \begin{matrix} a & b & \cdots & c \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ e & f & \cdots & g \end{matrix} $$

$$ \begin{bmatrix} a & b & \cdots & c \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ e & f & \cdots & g \end{bmatrix} $$

$$ \begin{pmatrix} a & b & \cdots & c \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ e & f & \cdots & g \end{pmatrix} $$

$$ \begin{vmatrix} a & b & \cdots & c \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ e & f & \cdots & g \end{vmatrix} $$ 矩阵应该用加粗的直立体表示 $$ \bf A ,\bf B^{\rm{T}} $$
