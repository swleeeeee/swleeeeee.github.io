Issue 3: LOGIC

  1. 以读者的位置去思考公式推导的流畅性，可行性强的方法就是费曼法。

  2. 在论文自行推导的过程中需要让每一步都有理有据，不能有任何跳跃，这样方便出问题后的检阅确认，特别是老师提出质疑的时候。而在整理成论文时则可以有所取舍

  3. 实验图部分的分析重点就是两个：实验现象（包含具体现象和误差来源）、分析讨论（包含仿真、理论的有效性分析，实验说明的特性以及他与目标功能的联系） 叙述逻辑需要非常清晰，建议是不要盯着其中某个值（如结构的泊松比图）在一段里面进行实验现象说明与分析讨论，建议分开

Issue 3: Low-level Mistakes

  1. 图表缺失；
  2. 尺寸有无明显错误；
  3. 联立公式的格式；
  4. 微分符号d和d之间不要搞混了
  5. 公式的化简
  6. 单位有没有出错（比刚度）
  7. 字体、间距、公式左对齐
  8. 单个方程Eq. 多个方程Eqs.(1)-(3)
  9. 图片字体尺寸要和图片标题相一致.
  10. 后面推导没有用上的符号原则上不要标出来，奥卡姆剃刀

Issue 6: Introduction Storyline

How to tell a good sci story?

Soft network composite materials with deterministic and bio-inspired designs as an example:

  1. advanced synthetic materials( Research Background)
  2. Dificiencies in the existing study
  3. Bio-inspired (how did you come up with this idea, the key concept of the paper)
  4. tissue engineering and biomedical devices (Who has a high demand for it )

From Background,Dificiencies,Design Concepts,Engineering Requirements to Detailed Research， tell a story through to the end.

As for introduction, just have to put literature reviews forward.

  1. Background: (rectified)Honeycomb—honeycomb with other matrix—CFRP honeycomb
Honeycomb materials have emerged as pivotal lightweight solutions across engineering disciplines, offering exceptional specific stiffness, and energy absorption capabilities [1]. Their high porosity further allows multifunctional integration, including thermal management [2], electromagnetic stealth [3] and acoustic insulation [4].           Recent developments reveal distinct evolutionary trajectories across material systems. Metallic honeycombs, leveraging their superior plastic deformation capacity, dominate energy absorption and vibration damping applications [5]. Owing to their their cost-effectiveness and manufacturing simplicity, aramid honeycombs maintain widespread use in non-critical applications despite their inherent mechanical limitations [6]. At the cutting edge of materials technology, carbon fiber-reinforced polymer (CFRP) composite honeycombs synergize the superiorities of carbon fibers with optimized cellular geometries, making them ideal candidates for aerospace applications, particularly where ultralightweight and high thermal stability are essential design requirements [7, 8].

(original)CFRP honeycomb —curved CFRP honeycomb panels

As a combination of advanced structural materials and high-performance structure, CFRP honeycomb composites, which feature ultra-lightweight properties, extremely low thermal expansion coefficients, and high reliability, represent an ideal choice for achieving lightweight structures in large aerospace vehicles. Significant progress has been made in the research of CFRP honeycomb composites, leading to practical applications in fields such as aerospace. Complex devices impose specific requirements on the morphology of CFRP honeycomb composites.This, in turn, necessitates further exploration of curved honeycomb panels. In case that existing researches mainly focuses on flat plates, while studies on curved structures, which are widely used in spacecraft, are limited due to the constraints of manufacturing processes.

In this section, we have to focus on the positive and macro side of the field. 大背景，介绍的内容需要更加泛一些，就是不用具体切入到研究的方向上，重要的是要让人家看到这个研究的价值点/领域热点，像是上面的curved CFRP honeycomb 就太细化了，实际上只需要讲到CFRP honeycomb的研究热点、领域优势就可以。
